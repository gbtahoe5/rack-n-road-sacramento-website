Real Questions, Real Answers

Expert answers

Can my vehicle carry two 70-pound e-bikes?

Most traditional bike racks max out at 35–40 lbs per bike, while e-bikes commonly weigh 50–70 lbs. We carry Küat, Thule and Saris racks rated specifically for e-bike weight — some up to 100 lbs per tray — and we match the rack to your hitch class and vehicle so the whole system is rated for the load, not just the trays.

Should I use a roof rack or a hitch rack?

It depends on what you carry, how often, and who is lifting it. Hitch racks load lower and handle heavier bikes; roof systems keep the rear of the vehicle clear and carry boxes, boats and tents too. We look at your vehicle, your gear and your routine, and recommend the platform you'll actually use.

Can you install a hitch on my vehicle?

Almost certainly. We install trailer hitches every day — exposed receivers, hidden hitches and flush stealth-style systems — along with 4-pin and 7-pin wiring. Call or text with your year, make and model and we'll confirm fit and quote the install. Many hitch installs are completed same-day.

What size hitch do I need?

Class I and II handle bike racks and light cargo. Class III covers most small trailers, boats and campers. Class IV and V are for heavy towing. We match the hitch class to your vehicle and what you actually plan to tow or carry — not to whatever happens to be on the shelf.

Thule vs. Yakima — which is better for my vehicle?

Both are excellent, and the honest answer depends on your vehicle's roof, your gear and how the pieces combine. We're an authorized dealer and installer for both, we fit them daily, and we'll tell you which one genuinely suits your setup — including when the cheaper option is the right one.

Can I put a rooftop tent on my vehicle?

Most SUVs, trucks and crossovers can — it depends on your vehicle's dynamic roof load rating. Rooftop tents weigh 120–165 lbs plus the rack system, and factory crossbars usually aren't strong enough. We check your vehicle's specs and recommend the right rack platform before you spend a dollar on the tent.

Do you sell used racks?

Yes. We take trade-ins on Yakima and Thule systems and resell them at a discount when they're in good condition. Used inventory changes weekly — call, text or stop in to see what's available.

Do you have discontinued products?

Often, yes. Three decades in this business means towers, mounts and components that haven't been manufactured in years. If you're hunting for something discontinued, ask — there's a good chance we have it or know how to get it.

Can you find replacement parts for older racks?

We stock towers, clips, locks, end caps, rubber strips, crossbar parts and replacement keys for current and many discontinued Yakima and Thule systems. If it's not on the shelf, we can usually source it.

Do you install what you sell?

Yes — professional installation is the core of this business, and it has been since 1991. Hitches, wiring, roof systems, bike racks, truck systems and overlanding builds, installed correctly the first time by our in-house technicians.
