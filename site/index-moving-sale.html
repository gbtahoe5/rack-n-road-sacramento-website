Can my vehicle support a rooftop tent?

Most SUVs, trucks, and crossovers can — but it depends on your vehicle's dynamic roof load rating. Rooftop tents weigh 120 to 165 lbs plus the rack system. Factory crossbars usually are not strong enough. We check your vehicle's specs and recommend the right rack platform to support the tent safely. Customers from Auburn, Granite Bay, and Lincoln come in for rooftop tent consultations regularly.

Can a regular bike rack carry an e-bike?

Most traditional bike racks max out at 35 to 40 lbs per bike. E-bikes commonly weigh 50 to 70 lbs. We carry Kuat, Thule, and Saris racks specifically rated for e-bike weight — some up to 100 lbs per tray. If you don't have a hitch, we can install one and mount the rack in the same visit.

What class trailer hitch do I need?

It depends on what you are towing or carrying. Class I and II handle bike racks and light cargo. Class III covers most small trailers, boats, and campers. Class IV and V are for heavy towing. We match the hitch class to your vehicle and your intended use — not everyone needs the heaviest option. Learn more about hitch classes.

Can any hitch carry a bike rack?

No — and this is the one thing online listings won’t tell you. A hitch has two different ratings: towing weight and tongue weight, and a bike rack lives entirely on the tongue. A hitch rated to tow thousands of pounds may carry far less vertical weight at the receiver — and two e-bikes on a platform rack can push right past it. We match the hitch to the rack and the load first, then install it. That’s the difference between a hitch and a hitch chosen for your vehicle.

What is the difference between a hidden hitch and a standard hitch?

A hidden or concealed hitch tucks behind your bumper so you don't see the receiver when it's not in use. Stealth Hitches take it further with a completely flush factory-look finish. Standard exposed hitches are visible but usually less expensive. We install both and can show you samples in the shop. See all hitch options.

What is the difference between 4-pin and 7-pin trailer wiring?

4-pin handles basic lights — turn signals, brake lights, and running lights. 7-pin adds power for electric brakes, a 12V charging circuit for trailer batteries, and a reverse light circuit. If you are towing anything with brakes or a battery, you need 7-pin. We install both.

How long does a DECKED drawer system take to install?

About 2 to 3 hours for a full-size truck, slightly less for midsize. No drilling into your bed — DECKED systems bolt to existing tie-down points. You can still haul full sheets of plywood on top. We are an authorized DECKED installer and carry them in stock.

What is better — a retractable or hard folding tonneau cover?

Retractable covers like Retrax roll into a canister and give you full bed access with one hand. Hard folding covers like BAK fold toward the cab in panels and are usually less expensive. Retractable is better for frequent bed access. Hard folding is great for security on a budget. We carry both and can show you the difference in person.

What are electric running boards?

AMP Research PowerSteps extend automatically when you open the door and retract flush under the vehicle when you close it. They look factory, preserve ground clearance for off-road, and make getting in and out of lifted trucks much easier. We do plug-and-play installs on most trucks and SUVs.

Do you install racks, hitches, or gear I bought somewhere else?

Yes. Bring it or buy it here — it gets installed correctly either way. We install customer-supplied hitches, racks, cargo boxes, tents, and truck accessories every week. If you already did the research and bought the product, we’ll make sure it’s fitted, torqued, and safe on your vehicle.

How long does a trailer hitch installation take?

Most hitch installations take one to two hours, and many are completed same-day. Adding 4-pin or 7-pin wiring extends the visit slightly. Call or text ahead with your year, make, and model so we can confirm the hitch is in stock and ready when you arrive.

Will an aftermarket hitch or rack void my factory warranty?

No. Under federal law, a dealer can’t void your warranty just because an aftermarket hitch or rack was installed — a claim can only be denied if the accessory itself caused the problem. That’s exactly why professional installation matters: done right, there’s nothing for a dealer to point at.

Can I put a hitch on a leased vehicle?

Usually, yes. Most modern hitches are vehicle-specific, bolt to factory mounting points with no drilling, and can be removed cleanly before lease return. Check your lease terms, then bring us the vehicle — we’ll recommend a removable setup that leaves no trace.

Will a roof rack hurt my gas mileage or make noise?

Some, and we’ll be straight with you about it. Empty crossbars cost a little; a loaded cargo box at highway speed costs more — independent testing shows anywhere from a couple percent on SUVs to double digits on sedans. Low-profile boxes, wind fairings, and removing the system when you’re not using it all help. We set every install up so you can take it on and off easily.

How fast can I drive with a cargo box on my roof?

Normal highway speeds are fine. Most manufacturers recommend staying at or under roughly 80 mph with a loaded box, and every box has a weight limit we’ll review with you. We torque everything to spec and walk you through loading before you leave.

Do roof racks fit cars without factory rails?

Yes. Vehicles with bare “naked” roofs use vehicle-specific fit kits that clamp securely at the door frames or factory fixed points — no drilling, no damage. This is one of the most common installs we do. Tell us your year, make, and model and we’ll confirm the exact kit.

Can I take my rack or cargo box through a car wash?

No — automated car washes and roof systems don’t mix. Remove the box and accessories first, or hand-wash the vehicle. If a wash or low clearance ever damages your box, text us a photo; cracks can often be repaired.

Can I text you pictures of my vehicle or gear for advice?
