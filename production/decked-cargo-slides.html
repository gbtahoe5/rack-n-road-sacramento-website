Fitment & Compatibility

Will DECKED fit my truck? DECKED makes systems for most full-size and midsize trucks — Ford, Chevy/GMC, Ram, Toyota, Nissan, Rivian, and more. Every system is router-cut for a specific make, model, year, and bed length. We'll confirm fitment before you buy.

Does it work with my tonneau cover or bed liner? DECKED is compatible with spray-in bed liners and most tonneau covers. It's not compatible with drop-in plastic liners, 5th wheel/gooseneck setups, or stepside/flareside beds. We'll verify your specific setup before installation.

Can I still use my full truck bed with DECKED installed? Yes. The deck surface supports up to 2,000 lbs (1,000 lbs midsize). Lumber, ATVs, furniture — whatever you normally haul goes on top. Your organized storage lives underneath in the drawers.

Is DECKED really weatherproof? Yes. Rain, hail, sleet, snow, car washes — nothing gets into the drawers. DECKED uses protective closeout panels that keep dust and moisture out completely. This is one of the most tested claims in the product line, backed by 9,700+ reviews confirming it holds up.

What is DECKED made from? High-impact resistant polymer resin (HDPE) co-molded to a galvanized steel subframe. The same materials used in industrial equipment — impervious to solvents, lubricants, UV, and weather. Made in the USA in Ohio.

How long does installation take? About 2–3 hours for a DIY install. Our technicians handle it faster. If you're adding a SmartCap, rack system, or other accessories on the same visit, plan for a half-day appointment. Once installed, DECKED removes in about 15 minutes if needed.

Is DECKED worth the investment? With 9,700+ reviews averaging 4.9 stars, a lifetime warranty, and American-made construction — owners overwhelmingly say yes. Come see one in person at any Rack N Road location and decide for yourself. We're happy to walk through an honest comparison with any alternative.

Do you have DECKED systems in stock? We're a full stocking authorized dealer — popular configurations for Ford, Chevy/GMC, Ram, and Toyota are typically in stock across our 8 locations. Less common applications may require ordering. Call for real-time availability before making the drive.

What's the warranty? DECKED backs everything with a Limited Lifetime Warranty. Our installation is backed by our own guarantee — if there's ever an issue with how we installed it, we'll make it right. No runaround, no questions asked.
